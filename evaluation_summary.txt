MODEL EVALUATION SUMMARY
========================

Base Model:
distilgpt2

Fine-Tuned Model:
DistilGPT-2 fine-tuned on the Databricks Dolly instruction dataset

Training Dataset:
Approximately 2,000 instruction-response examples

Evaluation Method:
Both models were tested on the same set of instruction-based prompts.

Observations:
1. The base model often generated generic or incomplete text.
2. The fine-tuned model produced responses more related to instructions.
3. The fine-tuned model sometimes generated list-like and task-oriented answers.
4. Some responses remained repetitive or incomplete.
5. The model's limitations are likely related to the small training dataset,
   model size, and limited fine-tuning steps.

Conclusion:
Fine-tuning improved the model's instruction-following behavior to some
extent, but the model is not yet suitable for production use.
